🚨 Common traps
❌ Premolars are anterior teeth
✅ Anterior = incisors + canine. Premolars and molars are posterior.
❌ The deciduous dentition contains premolars
✅ Each deciduous quadrant has 2 incisors + 1 canine + 2 molars (5 teeth) and no premolars.
❌ The mixed dentition starts when the first deciduous tooth falls
✅ It starts with the eruption of the 1st permanent molar and ends when the last deciduous tooth falls.
❌ The deciduous molars are replaced by molars
✅ They are replaced by premolars. The 3 permanent molars have no predecessors.
❌ The dental formula shows the whole mouth
✅ It shows one side only (upper + lower): total = formula × 2.
❌ In the International system, deciduous teeth start with 1 – 4
✅ The first number is 1 – 4 for permanent and 5 – 8 for deciduous teeth.
❌ In the Universal system, #1 is the upper right central incisor
✅ #1 is the upper right 3rd molar. The upper right central incisor is #8.
❌ Palmer uses letters for the permanent teeth
✅ Palmer: numbers 1 – 8 for permanent teeth, letters A – E for deciduous teeth.

① Crown, root & tissues — Macro-anatomy of the tooth
TermDefinition
Anatomical crown (التاج التشريحي)Part of the tooth covered by enamel, up to the cemento-enamel junction (CEJ).
Clinical crown (التاج السريري)Part of the tooth that is visible in the mouth.
Anatomical root (الجذر التشريحي)Part of the tooth covered with cementum.
Clinical root (الجذر السريري)Part of the tooth that is not visible in the mouth.
Cervical lineSeparates the crown from the root.
💡 يعني إيه؟ Anatomical يعني بيتحدد بالنسيج اللي بيغطي الجزء، وClinical يعني بيتحدد باللي ظاهر في الفم. لو اللثة نزلت وظهر جزء من الجذر، يفضل Anatomical root بس بيبقى جزء من الـ Clinical crown.
② Crown, root & tissues — Micro-anatomy of the tooth
TissueDescription
Enamel (المينا)The hardest tissue in the body; covers the anatomical crown.
Dentin (العاج)Hard tissue forming the main bulk of the tooth. It surrounds the pulp cavity (تجويف اللب), and is covered by enamel in the crown and by cementum in the root.
CementumLayer of hard, bone-like tissue covering the anatomical root.
Pulp (اللب)Living soft tissue occupying the pulp cavity of a vital tooth; contains the blood vessels and the nerve supply.
💡 يعني إيه؟ ترتيب الطبقات من برة لجوه: التاج Enamel → Dentin → Pulp، والجذر Cementum → Dentin → Pulp.

Oblique ridgeCrosses the occlusal surface obliquely, from the distobuccal to the mesiolingual cusp. Found in maxillary molars (E, 6 & 7).
⑬ Crown depressions — Grooves & Fissure
TermDescription
Developmental grooveShallow linear depression that usually marks the coalescence (اتحاد) of the primary parts (lobes) of the crown. Found on the occlusal surfaces, and may extend to the buccal and lingual surfaces of posterior teeth.
Supplemental grooveBranches from a developmental groove. Its location is not related to the junction of the primary parts, and it is normally not as deep as a primary groove.
FissureLinear depression at the bottom of a developmental groove; results from incomplete union of primary lobes, so it is a fault in enamel.
💡 يعني إيه؟ التسوس (dental caries) غالبًا بيبدأ في الـ fissure العميق.
⑭ Crown depressions — Fossa, Pit & Sulcus
TermDescription
FossaIrregular, usually rounded depression (concavity) on the crown. Named from its place: lingual fossa (lingual surface of anterior teeth) · mesial and distal triangular fossae (occlusal surface of posterior teeth, next to the marginal ridges) · central fossa (occlusal surfaces of molars, at the junction of the developmental grooves).
PitSmall depressed area in the deepest portion of a central fossa, or at the bottom of the triangular fossae. A faulty pit results from incomplete formation of enamel; located at the end of the buccal developmental grooves of lower molars, or the palatal developmental grooves of upper molars.
SulcusBroad depression or valley on the occlusal surface of posterior teeth.
💡 يعني إيه؟ Sulcus يعني منخفض واسع زي الوادي، وGroove يعني خط ضيق. Pit أصغر من Fossa وجواها.
⑮ Crypt & socket — Definitions
TermDescription
CryptA bony space in the alveolar bone (عظم الفك) containing the developing, unerupted tooth.
SocketA bony space in the alveolar bone containing the roots of an erupted tooth.
💡 يعني إيه؟ Crypt يعني مكان السن وهو لسه جوه العظم ومطلعش. Socket يعني مكان جذور السن اللي طلع.

① General features — Mandibular incisors
TopicKey facts
NumberFour in number, two on each side from the midline.
Lateral incisor positionThe mandibular lateral incisors are distal to the central incisors. The lateral makes contact mesially with the central incisor and distally with the canine.
Central vs LateralSimilar in anatomy and complement each other in function, but the central is somewhat smaller than the lateral.

③ Mandibular Central Incisor — Labial aspect

في السفلية السمك LL أكبر، وفي العلوية العرض MD أكبر.
Central vs lateral
Central = Symmetrical · Lateral = Twisted
The mandibular central is bilaterally symmetrical; the lateral is tilted distally and shows the distolingual twist.
الـ central جانبيه شبه بعض، أما الـ lateral فمايل وفيه لفّة.
Distolingual twist
Incisal + Mesial
Best seen from the incisal and mesial aspects, not the distal aspect.
بتبان اللفّة من الـ incisal والـ mesial مش من الـ distal.
